# 06 - The Girl Who Became Everyone's Story
by Obinna D. Ugoeke, Snr

   ![The girl who became everyone’s story](154aa940-9c07-4dfc-ab9d-32ec2baaa8e6.jpeg)

She never got to tell her own story. Everybody else told it for her.

In the market, she was one story. In the church, she was another story. In her school, she was another story. On the internet, she was another story.

"She did this." "She said that." "She is like this." "She is like that."

Nobody asked her. Nobody said, "What really happened?" They just added their own chapter and passed it on.

That is how a girl becomes everyone's story except her own.

She made one mistake. One. And that mistake became her name. People forgot her real name. They started calling her by what happened to her.

She tried to explain. But explanation sounds like excuse when people have already decided who you are.

So she went quiet. She stopped defending herself. She let them talk. Because she learned a painful truth: people who enjoy your story will never want the true version. The true version is not interesting enough. The lie is sweeter.

Years later, she left. She went where nobody knew her name. Where nobody knew her story. And for the first time, she could breathe.

She was not the girl from that story anymore. She was just a girl. A human being. Starting again.

If you have ever been that girl — or that boy — hear this: you are not the story they told about you. You are the story you are still writing.

Let them keep their version. It was never about you. It was about what they needed to believe to feel better about themselves.

Your real story is still yours. And one day, you will tell it in your own voice, in your own time.

And when you do, the world will finally hear the truth: you were never the villain in their story. You were just the victim of their storytelling.
